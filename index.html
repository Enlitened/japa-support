A calm counter for prayers, mantras, affirmations, sacred texts and breath practices. For iPhone and iPad.

Common questions

Where is my practice data stored?
Only on your device. Japa has no account, no server and no network code at all — nothing you write ever leaves your iPhone or iPad.
Will my practices sync between devices?
Not yet. Each device keeps its own library. Your data is included in an iPhone or iPad backup, so it survives moving to a new device.
I reset today's count by accident. Is my history gone?
No. Resetting only clears today; every earlier day stays exactly as it was.
Can I set a goal other than 108?
Yes. Pick 9, 21, 54 or 108, or type any number you like in the custom goal field.
How do I change the language or appearance?
In Settings, inside the app. Japa is available in English and Burmese, and can follow the system appearance or be pinned to light or dark.
Does Japa work with VoiceOver?
Yes. The counter, the day summary and the library are all labelled for VoiceOver, and Japa supports Dynamic Type and Reduce Motion throughout.
How do I delete everything?
Deleting a practice removes it and its history. Deleting the app removes everything Japa has stored.
