Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 August 2026

Japa does not collect any data about you.

What Japa stores

Everything you create in Japa — your practices, their names, the words you write or paste, your daily goals, your counts and your history — is stored only on your device, in the app's own local database. Your appearance and language preferences are stored the same way.

What Japa sends

Nothing. Japa contains no network code. It cannot send your practices, your words or your counts anywhere, because it never connects to the internet at all.

What Japa does not do

  • No account, sign-in or email address is ever requested.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage measurement.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking, and no data shared with any third party for any purpose.
  • No access to location, contacts, photos, microphone or health data.

Backups

If you back up your device to iCloud or to a computer, your Japa data is included in that backup, under Apple's terms and encryption rather than ours. Japa does not manage or read those backups.

Deleting your data

Deleting a practice inside Japa removes it and its recitation history from your device. Deleting the app removes everything Japa has stored.

Children

Japa is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy
