Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 September 2026

Japa does not collect any data about you.

What Japa stores

Everything you create in Japa — your practices, their names, the words you write or paste, your daily goals, your counts, how long you practised and your history — is stored on your device, in the app's own local database. Your appearance and language preferences are stored the same way.

If you turn on iCloud sync (off unless you turn it on, and part of Japa Lifetime), that same data is also kept in your own private iCloud database, so your other devices signed into the same Apple Account show the same practices and the same history. It goes to Apple's iCloud under your Apple Account and Apple's terms; it is never visible to us, and Japa has no server of its own to send it to. Turning sync off stops the mirroring; deleting the iCloud copy is done in iOS Settings under your Apple Account's iCloud storage, like any app's.

Japa's widgets and its Live Activity read a copy of today's summary — practice names, today's counts, your streak — that the app keeps in a shared folder on the device for them. Nothing in that folder leaves the device.

What Japa sends

Nothing about you. Japa has no server, no account and no network code of its own. It cannot send your practices, your words, your counts, or anything else anywhere — with iCloud sync on, Apple's own CloudKit carries your data between your devices through your private iCloud, and nowhere else.

The one exception is the App Store itself: if you buy Japa Lifetime, Apple's own StoreKit talks to Apple to complete and verify the purchase. That conversation is between you and Apple; it carries nothing about your practices, and its result never leaves the device.

Purchases

Japa offers one optional purchase, Japa Lifetime, which saves an unlimited number of practices, opens your whole history and allows iCloud sync. It is handled entirely by the App Store.

Japa never sees your name, your Apple Account, your payment details or your purchase history. It asks the App Store one question — whether this device is entitled to Japa Lifetime — and the App Store answers on the device. Nothing about the purchase is sent to us or to anyone else, because there is nowhere to send it: Japa still has no server and no network code of its own.

What Japa does not do

  • No account, sign-in, or email address is ever requested.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or usage measurement.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking, and no data shared with any third party for any purpose.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone, or health data is accessed.

Backups

If you back up your device to iCloud or a computer, your Japa data is included in that backup, under Apple's terms and encryption rather than ours. Japa does not manage or read those backups.

Deleting your data

Deleting a practice inside Japa removes it and its recitation history from your device — and, with iCloud sync on, from your private iCloud and your other devices. Deleting the app removes everything Japa has stored on the device; the iCloud copy, if you made one, is yours to delete under your Apple Account.

Children

Japa is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, children included.
